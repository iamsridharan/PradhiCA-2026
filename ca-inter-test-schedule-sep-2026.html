DOT Marathon

Exam Starts: 17.05.2026 | 10 Weekly Test Series + Model Exams

Registration in Progress Weekly Schedule

DOT Marathon is a systematically devised test series that divides the entire course syllabus into series of 10 Weekly Test Series along with Model Exams. Registration Still in Progress. Students can register from this Week as first week.

Flexible Structure

A well-structured study plan, conducted over three months, can significantly enhance your exam preparation. Students can choose to write Both Groups or Groupwise or 2 Papers in a Groups.

ICAI Pattern

Exam Package includes 9 Weekly Chapter wise, 2 monthly revision and 2 Full syllabus 100 marks Model Exam. Exams are conducted as per new Scheme in 70 : 30 MCQ Pattern.

Flexible Timings

Exam is split into two sessions on each Sunday
Session I: 10:00am - 1:00pm
Session II: 2:00pm - 5:00pm
Note: Students can write the test later also. Not compulsory to write on the same date

Quick Results

Papers will be evaluated by qualified CAs. The results will be declared within 4 days with detailed performance analysis.

Mode

We offer both Direct (in-person) and Online modes. Students can choose to write the test remotely from home or in-person at our exclusive Chennai centre. Additionally, students registered for the Direct mode retain the flexibility to switch and write their exams via the Online mode, if necessary.

Dedicated Mentorship & Planning

A structured study plan is provided for every subject, detailing chapter-wise weightage, essential topics, and a daily preparation schedule. Our dedicated team conducts frequent follow-ups and is readily available to provide preparation guidance and expert tips upon student request
