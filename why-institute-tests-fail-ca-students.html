Why Institutes Can't (Or Won't) Fix These Problems

You might wonder why institutes don't improve their test series quality. The truth is shocking:

1. Test Series is Not Their Core Business

Institutes make money from coaching classes, not test series. Test series is just an add-on to justify higher package prices. They have no incentive to invest in quality improvement.

2. Hiring Qualified Evaluators is Expensive

Qualified CAs charge high fees for evaluation. To maintain profit margins, institutes use cheap, unqualified staff for checking answer sheets.

3. Students are Trapped in Packages

Once you've paid for the full package, institutes know you won't leave mid-course. They have no competitive pressure to improve quality.

4. Lack of Specialization

Institutes try to do everything - coaching, test series, study materials, doubt sessions. They can't specialize and excel in any one area.
