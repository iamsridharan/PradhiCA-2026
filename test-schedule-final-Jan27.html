DOT MARATHON - Exam Starts from 11.01.2026

DOT Marathon is a test series that includes 9 weekly chapter-wise scheduled tests, along with 2 monthly revision exams and 2 full-syllabus model exams conducted over a 4-month period.

Compatibility

A well-structured study plan, conducted over three months, can significantly enhance your exam preparation. Students can choose to write Both Groups or Groupwise or 2 Papers in a Groups.

Exam Pattern

Exam Package includes 9 Weekly Chapter wise, 2 monthly revision and 2 Full syllabus 100 marks Model Exam. Exams are conducted as per new Scheme in 70 : 30 MCQ Pattern.

Exam Timings

Exam is split into two sessions on each Sunday [Session I - 10.00am- 1.00pm - Session II 2.00pm to 5.00pm]
Note: Students can write the test later Also. Not Compulsory to write on the Same Date

Results

Papers will be Evaluated by Qualified CA's. The results will be declared within 4 Days.

Mode

We offer both Direct (in-person) and Online modes. Students can choose to write the test remotely from home or in-person at our exclusive Chennai centre. Additionally, students registered for the Direct mode retain the flexibility to switch and write their exams via the Online mode, if necessary.

Dedicated Mentorship & Planning
